📋 Methodology & Computation Documentation

  • Eligibility Denominator: Strictly includes students with CGPA ≥ 6.00, 0 active backlogs, and enrolled for campus placement.
  • Multiple Offers De-duplication: A candidate holding multiple offers is counted exactly once in the Placed Count.
  • Package Metric (LPA): All compensation numbers are normalized to Lakhs Per Annum.
  • Privacy Guardrail: Individual names and test scores are suppressed in this reporting view.
